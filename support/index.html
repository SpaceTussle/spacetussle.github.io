SPACE TUSSLE · SUPPORT

Two ships, a few planets, and nothing to stop you but inertia. Space Tussle is a vector space duel for iPhone, iPad and Mac, drawn in green phosphor like the oscilloscope screens of the 1960s, and inspired by Spacewar!, the game written at MIT in 1962.

Playing

  • Turn, thrust and fire. There is no brake: your ship keeps moving until you thrust the other way.
  • Planets pull ships and torpedoes alike. Touching one destroys your ship.
  • Ships wrap around the edges of the arena; torpedoes and lasers stop there.
  • Play against the computer at four levels, or against a friend on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Touch controls on iPhone and iPad; keyboard and game controllers on every device.

Local network games

Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network and allow Space Tussle to use it. If you declined the permission, turn it back on in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network (on Mac, System Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network). Some public or office networks block devices from seeing each other.
