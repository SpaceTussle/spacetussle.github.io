SPACE TUSSLE · PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Space Tussle is a game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. In short: Space Tussle collects no data about you.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect, store or send any personal information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code that tracks you.
  • It does not ask for an account, a name, an email address or a phone number.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

What stays on your device

The game keeps your settings (sound volumes, controls, display options) and your match statistics on your device only, so that you find them again the next time you play. They are never sent anywhere. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app deletes them.

Playing on a local network

When you choose to play with someone on the same Wi-Fi network, the game asks for permission to use the local network. It then finds the other players' devices directly and exchanges with them only what the match needs: the players' moves and the name of each device (for example "iPhone" or the name of your Mac), so that everyone can see who they are playing with. This traffic goes straight from device to device on your network. It does not pass through any server, and the developer never receives it.

Children

Since Space Tussle collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes anything described here, for example by adding online play through Apple's Game Center, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to spacetussle@icloud.com.
